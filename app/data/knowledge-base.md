# ShopWave support knowledge base

Grounding source for policy answers. The agent may quote from here; it may not invent
policy that is not written here.

## Return window

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Electronics have a 15-day window.
Hygiene and innerwear categories are non-returnable. The return deadline recorded on each
order is authoritative — if it conflicts with the general rule, the order record wins.

## Damaged or defective on arrival

Items that arrive damaged in transit are eligible for a full refund or a replacement at no
cost, including categories that normally carry a restocking fee. Report within the return
window. Photographs help but are not required to open the case.

## Refund timelines

Once a refund is approved, the amount is returned to the original payment method. Card and
UPI refunds typically settle in 5 to 7 working days. Net banking can take up to 10 working
days. ShopWave cannot control the settlement speed of the receiving bank.

## Refund approval limits

Refunds above the autonomous approval limit require a human support agent to authorise
them. The AI assistant will collect and verify the case details and hand over to a
colleague; it does not decide these itself and will not indicate a likely outcome.

## Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled while they are still in `processing` status. Once an order has
shipped it cannot be cancelled — it must be received and then returned.

## Replacements and exchanges

Size and colour exchanges are available within the return window for apparel and footwear,
subject to stock. Where stock is unavailable the case is converted to a refund.

## Delivered but not received

If tracking shows delivered and the customer did not receive the item, an investigation is
opened with the courier. Investigations typically close within 3 working days. A refund or
replacement is issued if the investigation cannot confirm delivery.

## Warranty

Warranty length is recorded per product. Warranty claims are handled by the manufacturer's
service network; ShopWave opens the claim and shares the reference number.

## Identity verification

Before any change is made to an order, the caller must confirm either the registered phone
number or the registered email address on the account.

## What the assistant will not do

The assistant does not give legal, financial or medical advice, does not promise delivery
dates it cannot verify, and does not confirm anything it has not checked against the order
record. Where a caller needs advice rather than support, it hands over to a human.
